# Fortis rolling PPL training log

The active prescription is the five-day rolling Push / Pull / Legs plan in `fortis-program.js` and `PPL_PROGRAM.md`. It replaces the four-core-session Fortis binder plan at the user's request. `fortis-program-legacy.js` preserves that 57-session plan and `fortis-program-ppl-v1.js` preserves the first PPL prescription; `program_notes.md` and older changelog entries are historical context, not active instructions.

## Constraints

- Static HTML, vanilla ES modules, mobile first; GitHub Pages hosting. Keep `index.html` and `powerbuilding.html` byte-identical.
- Twelve calendar weeks, five scheduled sessions each week, sixty sessions total. Cycle Push A / Pull A / Legs A / Push B / Pull B / Legs B continuously; do not reset weekly. Suggested days: Mon / Tue / Thu / Fri / Sat. All six variants have ten scheduled appearances.
- Main-lift variations: Push A regular bench 3×4–6 and Push B one-second paused bench 3×6–8 throughout. Legs A back squat in weeks 1–4/9–12, SSB box squat in weeks 5–8, 3×4–6. Legs B conventional floor deadlift in weeks 1–4/9–12, one-second paused conventional just off the floor in weeks 5–8, 2×3–5. Keep variations stable within blocks; no load-conversion percentages. Count the actual SSB bar weight and standardize a stable box with controlled contact, no rocking or relaxing.
- Deficit deadlift is not a default or app selector. It is only a separate coach/setup-checked low-deficit option with a stable full-foot platform and controlled position; do not silently log it under a programmed floor lift.
- Direct core: ab wheel 2×8–12, Push B cable crunch 2×10–15 at 2–3 RIR after the other work, and Pallof 2×10/side. Push B lateral raises are reduced to two sets; normal Push B total is thirteen work sets.
- Weeks 4, 8 and 12 are lighter, without restarting the rotation or changing the block variation: two main-lift sets, one per accessory at 4+ RIR. No optional fifth day: five training days are now the normal schedule.
- No plyometrics, explosive Olympic lifts, RDLs or front squats. Conventional deadlift is on Legs B. Current Fortis training was reported comfortable for knees/back; do not turn historical sensitivity notes into new diagnoses.
- Use the specified Fortis equipment and DB/cable substitutes. Do not assume incline/shoulder press machines, seated curls or standing calf machines exist. Equipment references: https://fortisfitness.ca/our-equipment/ and https://fortisfitness.ca/locations/fortis-fitness-east/ .
- Sixty-minute cap includes a scaled Wenning-inspired circuit, barbell ramps, rests and setup. Circuit plus ramps normally take 10–12 minutes. Lower-day unilateral curls take time; preserve main-lift rest and hamstring work, trimming the final leg-press/belt-squat/calf set when required.
- Main lifts: about 2 RIR and three-minute rests; secondary bench 2–3 RIR; accessories 2 RIR. No drops. Light weeks reduce work and use 4+ RIR.
- Circuit defaults: one easy round on every day at 8+ RIR and about 3–4/10 effort. A second upper round is optional only when useful, non-fatiguing and within the 10–12-minute preparation budget. Ramps must rehearse the actual paused/SSB/box variation. Circuit sets do not count as hard work sets. They do not replace main-lift ramps or supply missing hard-set muscle volume.
- Calibrate every lift manually; no inherited binder loads. Prescriptions and performed sets remain separate. Double progression follows actual successful performed exposures, never calendar dates or completion checkboxes. Do not silently increase load or reinterpret blank loads as zero-load prescriptions.
- Preserve legacy logs as read-only History. Keep storage/auth code and the existing `fortis-v1` account isolation unchanged. New record IDs must use `ppl-v2`; preserve `ppl-v1` history via its archive and keep PPL view selection separate from binder state. Main-lift IDs must include the variation slug so LAST comparisons cannot cross different variations. Never remap old records to new exercises or silently migrate logged loads.
- No sleep, bodyweight or session-note inputs. Workout data and credentials must not be committed to GitHub.
- Only public project configuration belongs in `fortis-config.js`; never expose a secret/service-role key. No cloud project is provisioned here; live Auth/policy/cross-device checks are required after setup.
- `npm test` must pass. Review mobile layout and persistence after relevant interface changes. Preserve meaningful legacy verification alongside new-program checks; do not rewrite the PDF-derived fixture to make a new prescription appear original.
