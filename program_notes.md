### Injury Management
- **Patellar tendonitis**: No front squats. No rebound plyometrics. Box jumps step down only. Leg extensions kept light (low weight, higher reps). Quads capped at ~16 sets/week. Box squats preferred variant (vertical shins reduce patellar stress).
- **Lower back**: No RDLs. All hinge accessory work is GHD hip extension or machine-based. Deficit deadlift replaced with block pull (reduces lumbar flexion demand). Push press replaced with seated barbell OHP / Viking press (removes knee loading from dip-drive).

## Program Architecture

### Philosophy: Wendler Forever Leader/Anchor + Conjugate Rotation

Two Leader cycles build work capacity with submaximal 5s PRO and higher supplemental volume (FSL 5x5). A 7th Week Protocol deload manages accumulated fatigue. Two Anchor cycles express strength with standard 5/3/1 AMRAP sets and reduced supplemental volume (FSL 3x5). Exercise rotation every cycle prevents accommodation while ramping specificity toward competition lifts in the final cycle.

Key upgrades from v1:
- Leader/Anchor periodization replaces flat 5/3/1 across all cycles
- Modified Wenning warmup (2 exercises x 3x25) replaces cold-start compounds
- Agonist-antagonist supersets on upper days save 8-10 min per session
- Intensity techniques (myo-reps, drop sets, rest-pause) compress accessory volume
- Hang clean pulls as deadlift power primer
- Structured accessory wave protocol replaces vague double progression
- Volume gaps fixed: biceps 6->12, side delts 6->9, triceps 3->12, back 13->21

**Leader cycles (wk 1-6)**: 5s PRO = all 3 working sets for 5 reps, no AMRAP. Builds volume without accumulating CNS fatigue from max-effort sets. FSL 5x5 provides high supplemental volume.

**7th Week Protocol (wk 7)**: 70% x5, 80% x3, 90% x1, 100% TM x1. No supplemental work. Light accessories only (2 sets each, reduced weight). Tests TM validity: the 100% TM single should move fast. If it grinds, reduce TM by 5%.

**Anchor cycles (wk 8-12)**: Standard 5/3/1 with AMRAP top sets (+). FSL drops to 3x5. This is where PRs happen. Accessories move to 50-100 reps per category (push/pull/SL-or-core).

**Peak mini-cycle (wk 11-12)**: Only 2 weeks at TM 92.5%. Week 11 = 5s week, Week 12 = 1s week. Competition lifts only. Natural strength peak. Option to attempt new 1RMs after completing prescribed sets in week 12.

### Anchors (Weeks 8-12)

FSL drops to 3x5 (from 5x5), reducing squat/bench/deadlift supplemental by 2 sets each. Per Wendler Forever, accessory categories move to 50-100 reps per category (push/pull/single-leg-or-core). Practically this means:
- Add 1 set to key accessories (4x instead of 3x) on exercises where you want more stimulus
- Or increase rep ranges (3x15 instead of 3x12)
- Myo-rep exercises can add 1 extra mini-set
- Net effect: main lift volume drops ~10%, accessory volume stays constant or increases slightly
- The freed time from FSL 3x5 (vs 5x5) provides room for the extra accessory volume

## Intensity Techniques Reference

### Myo-Reps
**What**: 1 activation set near failure (~10-12 reps), then 3-5 mini-sets of 3-5 reps with only 15-20s rest between. Stop when you can no longer match the mini-set rep count.
**Time**: ~2:00 total (replaces ~6:00 of 3 straight sets).
**Use on**: Cable fly, cable row, leg curl, lat pulldown, cable seated row, preacher curl, tricep pushdown, cable lateral raise, cable rear delt fly.
**Never use on**: Barbell compounds.

### Triple Drop Set
**What**: Perform ~10 reps at working weight. Immediately strip ~25% weight, perform ~8 reps. Strip again, perform ~8 reps. One continuous set.
**Time**: ~1:50 total (replaces ~5:00 of 3 straight sets).
**Use on**: Cable fly (D5), any machine or cable exercise.
**Never use on**: Free weights or barbell exercises.

### Rest-Pause
**What**: Perform ~8 reps at working weight. Rest 15s. Perform ~4 reps. Rest 15s. Perform ~3 reps. One extended set.
**Time**: ~1:55 total (replaces ~5:00 of 3 straight sets).
**Use on**: Hammer Strength incline press (D5), any machine compound.
**Never use on**: Barbell compounds or exercises requiring spotter.

### Agonist-Antagonist Superset
**What**: Alternate between opposing muscle groups (bench/pull-ups, OHP/chin-ups). Perform set of A, transition 15s, perform set of B, rest 2:00-2:30.
**Time**: 3:30 per round (vs ~5:00+ doing them sequentially).
**Use on**: D2 (bench + pull-ups), D4 (OHP + chin-ups).
**Benefit**: Saves 8-10 minutes per upper session. Reciprocal inhibition may actually enhance performance.
**Gym logistics**: If the gym is busy and you cannot hold both a bench and pull-up bar, do pull-ups AFTER the FSL block as a standalone 3x8-10. Adds ~5 min but eliminates equipment contention. Still fits under 60 min.

## Injury Management Protocols

### Patellar Tendonitis
- **Quad volume cap**: ~16 sets/week (at floor of MAV). Monitor -- reduce if flare-up.
- **No front squats**: All squat variants are back-loaded (SSB, box, pause, competition).
- **Box squats preferred**: Vertical shins at bottom reduce patellar stress. Used in Cycle 2.
- **No rebound plyometrics**: Box jumps are singles with step-down only. No depth jumps, no repeated jumps.
- **Leg extensions**: Light only. Used in Wenning warmup context (3x25 at ~20% 10RM) but NOT as a working accessory.
- **Pendulum squat**: Machine-based quad work with controlled eccentric. Preferred over leg press for knee-friendliness.
- **Sled push / Walking lunges**: Sled push is the default (zero eccentric knee loading). Walking lunges are the option ONLY if knees are fully asymptomatic. Controlled tempo, step through (not step back).

### Lower Back
- **No RDLs**: All hip hinge accessory work is GHD hip extension (bodyweight).
- **No deficit deadlifts**: Replaced with block pull (mid-shin) which reduces range of motion and lumbar flexion demand.
- **No push press**: Replaced with seated barbell OHP / Z Press / DB shoulder press.
- **Hang clean pulls**: Capped at 60% DL 1RM (120kg max). Explosive concentric, controlled return. Stop immediately if back tightens. Alternative: KB swings 3x5-8 at 32-40kg if technique is a barrier.
- **GHD hip extensions for warmup**: Bodyweight only on warmup sets (3x25). Working sets at 3x10-12 with slow eccentric.

## Autoregulation Guidelines

### Main Lifts (5s PRO -- Leaders)
- All 3 working sets are for 5 reps. No AMRAP. If you cannot complete 5 reps on any set, your TM is too high.
- These should feel controlled and fast. Bar speed is your feedback mechanism.
- If week 3 (95% TM x5) feels like a grinder, note it. The 7th Week Protocol will confirm.

### Main Lifts (5/3/1 -- Anchors)
- First 2 sets are prescribed reps. Hit them and move on.
- AMRAP top set: push for reps but stop 1-2 reps short of true failure. Leave 1-2 in the tank.
- Rep targets for "good" AMRAP performance:
  - 5+ week: aim for 7-10 reps
  - 3+ week: aim for 5-7 reps
  - 1+ week: aim for 3-5 reps
- If you cannot hit the minimum prescribed reps on the AMRAP, reduce 1RM by 5%.

### FSL Sets
- Should feel moderate. If the last FSL set is a grinder, something is wrong.
- On days you feel great: keep the rest periods as prescribed. Do NOT add weight or sets.
- On days you feel terrible: complete the sets. The submaximal load means you always can.

### Accessories
- Follow the wave protocol. Do not ego-lift on accessories.
- If you cannot complete the prescribed reps at a given weight, stay at that weight next session.
- Myo-rep sets: if you cannot match the previous mini-set rep count, stop. That is the built-in autoregulation.

### Deload Triggers (beyond Week 7)
- Sleep quality degraded for 3+ consecutive days
- Joint pain (not just DOMS) persisting more than 48 hours
- Missing prescribed reps on main lifts for 2+ sessions
- Motivation crater (a sign, not a cause -- usually means accumulated fatigue)
- Action: drop to deload protocol for that week's remaining sessions, then resume.
